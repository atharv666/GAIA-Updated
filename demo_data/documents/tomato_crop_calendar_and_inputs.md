# Tomato crop calendar and inputs (north plot)

This sheet is the farm's own plan for the north plot tomato crop, variety Arka Rakshak, a determinate
hybrid that is tolerant to several common diseases but not immune to early blight.

## Calendar
- Week 0 (2 September): transplanting at 45 cm x 60 cm, drip lines laid, mulch applied.
- Week 3: staking and first tying. First top dressing with a nitrogen and potassium mix through the drip.
- Week 5 to 6: flowering begins. Reduce nitrogen, increase potassium and calcium to limit blossom end rot.
- Week 8: first picking. Pick every second day when fruits turn about 75 percent red.
- Week 14 to 16: end of the main harvest. Uproot and burn badly diseased plants, do not compost them.

## Water
Tomato roots need even moisture. The target soil moisture band for this farm's capacitive probe is
35 to 60 percent. Below 25 percent the plants wilt at midday. Above 80 percent for more than a day raises
the risk of root rot and cracking. Drip irrigation runs every morning for 30 minutes.

## Nutrition
- Basal: farmyard manure 10 tonnes per acre mixed in before transplanting.
- Fertigation every 10 days with water soluble NPK, adjusted to the stage above.
- Foliar calcium nitrate spray at flowering if blossom end rot shows.

## Weather and disease watch
Warm days with humidity above 80 percent and leaf wetness favour early blight and late blight. After a rainy
night, scout the lower leaves for brown spots with rings and remove affected leaves. Do not irrigate by
sprinkler in the evening. Keep the rows open so air can move through the canopy.

## Records to keep
Date of each spray, product name, and the pre-harvest interval printed on the label.
