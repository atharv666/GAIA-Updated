PESTICIDE AND SPRAY SAFETY - STANDARD PROCEDURE (Green Acres)

1. Always read the label. Use only products registered for the crop, at the dose printed on the label.
2. Wear gloves, a mask, long sleeves and boots when mixing and spraying. Wash clothes separately afterwards.
3. Do not spray in strong wind, in the heat of the day, or when rain is expected within a few hours.
4. Respect the pre-harvest interval (PHI): do not pick fruit before the number of days stated on the label.
5. Never mix leftover spray into the drip system. Rinse the sprayer away from the irrigation channel and the well.
6. Prefer non-chemical steps first: remove infected leaves, improve spacing, mulch, avoid overhead watering.
7. Record the date, product, dose and field in the spray register.
8. If unsure, ask the local agriculture extension office (KVK) before spraying.
