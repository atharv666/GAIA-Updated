# Rover route and soil probe spots
Morning route (06:00-09:00): gate, north plot rows 1 to 6, then the south chilli beds.
Evening route (16:00-18:30): south beds, north plot, east corner near the biogas unit.
The soil probe is lowered at three fixed spots only while the rover is parked: north plot row 3, the middle
of the chilli beds, and the east corner. Readings while the rover is driving have no soil value by design.
